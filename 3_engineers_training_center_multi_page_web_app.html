Course Overview & Objectives

The **Professional BIM Diploma** is engineered to transform traditional engineers into BIM Specialists and Coordinators. You won't just learn tool buttons; you will execute full architectural and structural BIM modeling, establish BIM Execution Plans (BEP), resolve complex multi-disciplinary clashes in Navisworks, and automate tedious tasks using Dynamo.

Detailed Curriculum & Modules

Module 1: Revit Architecture & Modeling Standards

20 Hours
  • • Project setup, levels, grids, and shared coordinates.
  • • Parametric wall types, curtain walls, floors, roofs, and complex family creation.
  • • Room tags, area schedules, and sheet setup according to consultant standards.

Module 2: Revit Structure & Reinforcement

25 Hours
  • • Structural foundations, columns, beams, trusses, and shear walls.
  • • Rebar modeling (3D rebar, rebar sets, stirrups, and bar bending schedules - BBS).
  • • Analytical model checking and linking with structural analysis software.

Module 3: Navisworks Manage & Coordination

20 Hours
  • • Aggregating multi-disciplinary models (Arch, Struct, MEP).
  • • Automated Clash Detection matrix and clash resolution reports.
  • • 4D Construction Sequencing Simulation (TimeLiner) and 5D Quantity Take-off.

Module 4: Dynamo Scripting & BIM Management (ISO 19650)

15 Hours
  • • Visual programming basics in Dynamo for automated parameter editing & sheet creation.
  • • Understanding ISO 19650 terminology: CDE, EIR, BEP, LOD 100 to 400.
  • • Final Graduation Project: Full shop drawings submission for a multi-story building.

Course Overview

Designed specifically for structural and civil engineers seeking real-world consulting experience. This diploma covers full structural modeling, dynamic analysis, lateral stability under wind and earthquake forces, foundation design, and preparation of complete technical calculation reports for authority approvals.

Curriculum Modules

Module 1: Gravity Load Analysis & Slab Systems (SAFE)

25 Hours
  • • Modeling Solid, Flat, Hollow Block, and Paneled Beam Slab systems.
  • • Long-term deflection calculations and cracking verification.
  • • Punching shear verification and reinforcement detailing.

Module 2: High-Rise Seismic & Wind Analysis (ETABS)

35 Hours
  • • 3D building modeling, diaphragm assignment, and mass source setup.
  • • Equivalent Static & Response Spectrum seismic analysis methods.
  • • Shear wall design, core wall coupling beams, and drift/p-delta checks.

Module 3: Foundations & Soil-Structure Interaction

20 Hours
  • • Isolated, combined, and strip footing design.
  • • Raft on elastic foundation modeling and soil spring stiffness.
  • • Pile cap design and pile load distribution calculations.

Module 4: Steel Framing & Calculation Reports (SAP2000)

15 Hours
  • • Steel trusses, portal frames, and connection details.
  • • Exporting automated structural shop drawings via CSI Detail.
  • • Preparing structural calculation notes for consultant submittal.
