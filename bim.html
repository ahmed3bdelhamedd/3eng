BIM COMPREHENSIVE DIPLOMA

This diploma is designed to take you from absolute basics to professional mastery in Building Information Modeling (BIM). Learn architectural and structural workflows, clash detection, shop drawings, and estimation practically through real-world projects.

What Will You Learn?

  • • Complete understanding of BIM concepts and common data environments (CDE).
  • • Mastering Autodesk Revit Architecture from scratch to advanced modeling.
  • • Mastering Revit Structure for concrete and steel detailing.
  • • Managing coordination and clash detection using Navisworks.
  • • Creating accurate shop drawings and structural schedules.
  • • Quantity takeoff and cost estimation workflows.

Course Curriculum

Build a strong foundation in BIM standards, file linking, levels, grids, and project setup.
Walls, floors, roofs, stairs, curtain walls, and architectural detailing.
Foundations, columns, framing, concrete reinforcement, and structural framing setups.
Using Autodesk Navisworks Manage for interference checks and issue reporting.
Extracting professional sheets, schedules, tags, and construction documents.
